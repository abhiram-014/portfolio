Hey, this is Abhiram.

I’m an undergraduate student passionate about storytelling, video editing, and creating visuals that genuinely capture attention. I help brands transform ordinary content into visually engaging experiences designed to connect with audiences and improve retention.

Over the past 2 years, I’ve worked with 20+ brands and clients across 150+ projects while balancing both college and professional work — an experience that has strengthened my creativity, consistency, and time management skills.

I’m proficient in Adobe After Effects, Premiere Pro, and Photoshop, using them to craft content ranging from motion graphics and brand campaigns to cinematic edits and promotional reels.

If you’re looking to make your content more engaging, impactful, and visually refined, I’d be glad to help bring your ideas to life.
